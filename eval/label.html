Phase 09 labelling

40 days on which AAPL fell sharply. For each, mark every headline that explains that day's move, and write the cause in one line. Your work saves in this browser as you go; Export writes labels.json.

Rules (from docs/phase09-plan.md)
  • Relevant = the headline reports or explains this day's move in AAPL: company news (earnings, iPhone demand, China, lawsuits…) or market-wide news plausibly moving AAPL that day (Fed, inflation data, a broad tech sell-off).
  • Not relevant = listicles ("3 stocks to buy"), other companies' news, generic commentary, or news about a different day.
  • Cause: one plain line, e.g. "Hot CPI print triggered a broad tech sell-off". If nothing here explains the move, tick No headline explains this move.
  • Judge only from the headlines shown, not from what you know happened. Order is shuffled and the page doesn't say how each headline was found.
  • Keys: Y/N mark the next unanswered headline, →/← change day.
